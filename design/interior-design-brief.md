# Villa Interior Design Brief: Warm Minimal Luxury

Ground-floor interior scheme for the villa (28m × 31–35m plot) shown in the
ground floor plan, axonometric sketch and untextured Rhino model. The
architecture, openings and layout stay exactly as modelled. This brief covers
finishes, lighting, joinery and furniture only.

## House-wide rules

- **Direction:** contemporary luxury, warm minimalism, boutique-hotel
  residence. The materials carry the design, not ornament. Each main space gets
  exactly one sculptural "wow" element.
- **Base palette:** cream, sand, taupe, greige. **Grounding tones:** dark
  walnut, smoked bronze, charcoal. **Accents:** cognac/tan leather, burnt
  orange. **Rare accent:** one teal/blue-green note per room.
- **Continuous floor:** large-format (1200×2400) honed grey marble across all
  ground-floor living areas, with no thresholds. Polished finish at the
  entrance only.
- **Metal:** smoked bronze (PVD or patinated) for every decorative metal item.
  Satin black steel for window profiles and structural balustrade posts only.
- **Lighting temperature:** 2700K everywhere, on dimmable DALI scenes
  (Day / Evening / Entertain / Night). Hide every light source except the one
  sculptural fixture in each space.
- **Joinery:** dark walnut in quarter-cut veneer with a matt oil finish. Pale
  white oak is used for ceiling slats where a lighter tone is needed.

## 1. Double-height living (Double Void Living Room)

**Concept: "The Gallery Hall."** A calm, 7m-tall stone-and-timber volume that
opens to the pool garden. The book-matched marble wall is the backdrop, and the
glass chandelier is the event.

| Element | Material / finish |
|---|---|
| Feature wall (full height) | Book-matched Rainforest Brown marble, honed, 20mm slabs on a hidden subframe; 25mm shadow gap at the floor and top |
| Flanking walls | Vertical walnut slats (40×20 @ 60 c/c) over black acoustic felt |
| Other walls | Warm sand mineral lime plaster |
| Gallery/void edge | Frameless low-iron glass balustrade, smoked-bronze cap rail; walnut-clad slab edge fascia |
| Ceiling | Flush plaster, perimeter shadow-gap cove |
| Floor | Honed grey marble; hand-knotted sand wool rug, tone-on-tone |
| Glazing | Existing full-height steel profiles; sheer linen curtains in greige on a recessed track |

**Lighting plan**
- **Wow element:** a glass-shard cascade chandelier of clear and smoked glass,
  roughly 4.5m tall, suspended from the upper slab through the void.
- Grazing LED at the foot and head of the marble wall.
- A linear LED groove in the gallery slab soffit.
- Warm uplight into the walnut slats.
- Discreet trimless downlights only over the seating group.

**Key furniture**
- A low, deep modular sofa (about 3.6m, L-shaped) in cream bouclé.
- A pair of curved lounge chairs in cognac leather.
- A sculptural travertine coffee table.
- A burnt-orange mohair cushion.
- A teal ceramic vessel, art books, and a tall Strelitzia plant.

**Render prompt** (index 1 in the Higgsfield batch)
> Photorealistic architectural interior photograph of the DOUBLE-HEIGHT LIVING
> ROOM of the modern villa shown in the reference images (white Rhino model and
> ground floor plan). Keep the architecture exactly: a ~7m tall double-volume
> void, full-height floor-to-ceiling glazing on the left facing the garden and
> pool, the upper-floor gallery edge visible across the top of the void, slim
> black steel window profiles. Camera: eye level from the corridor side looking
> toward the garden glazing, 24mm lens. Interior design: contemporary luxury
> warm minimalism, boutique hotel residence. Full-height feature wall in
> book-matched Rainforest brown marble slab with vertical walnut slat panels
> either side and hidden LED reveals. Monumental suspended chandelier: cascade
> of clear and smoked glass shards falling through the void. Upper gallery edge
> with frameless glass balustrade capped in smoked bronze and a linear LED
> groove on the slab soffit. Floor: large-format honed grey marble; tone-on-tone
> sand wool rug. Low deep modular sofa in cream bouclé, two curved cognac
> leather lounge chairs, sculptural travertine coffee table, burnt orange
> cushion accent, one teal ceramic vessel, art books, tall tropical plant.
> Palette cream, sand, taupe, walnut, smoked bronze, charcoal. Soft
> late-afternoon daylight plus warm 2700K cove light. Editorial architectural
> photography, calm, material-led, no clutter, no people, no text.

## 2. Standard-height living (Living Room, rear, opening to Roofed Terrace)

**Concept: "The Library Lounge."** The intimate evening room, lower, darker and
warmer, set against the bright void. Its fire, books and timber ceiling draw
you in.

| Element | Material / finish |
|---|---|
| Ceiling | Dark walnut timber lining with recessed linear LED grooves |
| Fireplace wall | Textured terracotta clay plaster (hand-trowelled) |
| Fireplace | Linear gas fire (about 2.4m) set in a charcoal honed-granite plinth that runs the full wall |
| Library wall | Floor-to-ceiling dark walnut carcass, smoked-bronze framed shelving, backlit shelf edges |
| Floor | Honed grey marble; thick greige wool rug |
| Terrace glazing | Existing sliding steel doors; heavy linen curtains in taupe |

**Lighting plan**
- **Wow element:** the fireplace plinth itself, a single charcoal stone bar
  that glows.
- Backlit library shelving on its own circuit.
- Linear grooves in the walnut ceiling.
- A low picture light over the art.
- Table lamps on dim-to-warm (down to 2200K) for the night scene.

**Key furniture**
- A deep modular sofa in taupe linen.
- A curved cognac leather lounge chair with ottoman.
- Round walnut and travertine side tables.
- A burnt-orange throw and a small teal-glazed ceramic.

**Render prompt** (index 2)

> travertine side tables, burnt orange throw, one small teal-glazed ceramic.
> Warm 2700K layered hidden lighting, glow of fireplace, dusk blue light
> through the glazing. Editorial interior photography, no people, no text.

## 3. Kitchen (plus Dirty Kitchen behind)

**Concept: "The Stone Monolith."** The show kitchen is a quiet, handle-free
walnut enclosure around one sculptural island. All real cooking mess goes to
the dirty kitchen behind a flush jib door.

| Element | Material / finish |
|---|---|
| Island | Calacatta Viola marble monolith, 60mm mitred waterfall ends; a backlit honey-onyx serving section on the top |
| Tall units | Handleless (push/gola) dark walnut veneer, full height; integrated fridge, ovens and coffee |
| Display niche | Smoked-glass shelves in bronze frames, backlit, set centrally in the tall wall |
| Splashback / worktops (perimeter) | Vein-cut travertine, honed and filled |
| Ceiling | Pale oak slats with linear LED grooves |
| Floor | Honed grey marble (continuous) |
| Dirty kitchen | Charcoal matt lacquer units, stainless worktops; flush jib door clad to match the walnut |

**Lighting plan**
- **Wow element:** the backlit onyx top on the island.
- Three slim smoked-bronze linear pendants over the island.
- Backlit display niche.
- Under-cabinet task LED at 3000K on its own circuit.
- LED grooves in the oak ceiling.

**Key furniture**
- Three counter stools in cognac leather and bronze.
- Styling: one stone fruit bowl, a burnt-orange ceramic, a teal glass vase and
  an olive tree.

**Render prompt** (index 3)
> Photorealistic architectural interior photograph of the KITCHEN of the modern
> villa (the 'Kitchen' at front-left of the ground floor plan reference, with
> the Dirty Kitchen behind a door to its right and windows onto the garden).
> Keep architecture exactly: standard ~3m ceiling, rectangular room, garden
> window on the left wall, opening toward the bar and double-height dining.
> Camera: eye level, 24mm, from the dining/bar side looking into the kitchen.
> Design: contemporary luxury warm minimalism. Centrepiece: sculptural
> monolithic island in Calacatta Viola marble with thick waterfall ends and a
> backlit honey onyx top section glowing softly; three leather and bronze bar
> stools. Back wall: floor-to-ceiling handleless tall units in dark walnut
> veneer with integrated appliances, a central niche of backlit open display
> shelving with smoked-glass shelves and bronze frames holding ceramics.
> Ceiling in pale oak slats with linear LED grooves; three slim bronze linear
> pendants over the island. Floor large-format honed grey marble. Splashback in
> vein-cut travertine. Accents: cognac leather, a burnt orange bowl, a teal
> glass vase, tropical plant. Soft daylight plus warm hidden lighting.
> Editorial architectural photography, no people, no text.

**Key furniture**

## Revision 2: changes from the client's inspiration images

The client shared five Instagram references. I cropped them and used them as
style references only; the plan still sets the architecture. Each one changes
the scheme as follows:

| Ref | What we take from it | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Kitchen, travertine island (@jamal_zein_) | Vein-cut silver travertine monolith island, raised **backlit onyx bar block**, cognac leather stools with floor uplights, walnut framed tall units, charcoal rounded-panel base units, backlit travertine display shelving, **branching bronze chandelier with white glass globes** | Kitchen (replaces the Calacatta Viola island and linear pendants) |
| Dark kitchen/dining (nkey.architects) | Dark smoked-timber ceiling, **looping linear LED ring**, tall dark-bronze vertical fins with a top LED cove, Rainforest-brown stone, warm LED shelf strips | Library lounge, kitchen splashback |
| Fluted oak bar niche (sereenhassaniesharchitects) | Fluted light-oak wall with **backlit floating bar shelves**, a free-standing bronze tube screen, curved bouclé sofa and swivel chairs, smoked-glass and bronze coffee table | Library lounge, double-height living seating |
| Slatted screen and sideboard (Gunawan x Gunawan) | Dark bronze slatted screen with backlit shelves above a **curved light-oak cabinet with a green marble top** | Entrance side wall, dining sideboard |
| Villa triptych (Gunawan x Gunawan) | **Wave-carved walnut feature wall**, **teal and clear glass-shard chandelier**, granite gallery fascia, backlit labradorite bar; dark stone entry wall with a **gold ribbon wall light** and leaf-perforated screen; dining with a full bottle wall, glass lantern pendants and dark marble floor | Double-height living, entrance, dining |

### What changed in the scheme

- **Double-height living:** the Rainforest Brown marble wall becomes a
  **wave-carved walnut wall**. The shard chandelier turns teal and clear, and
  the gallery edge is clad in white-grey granite with a backlit labradorite
  bar niche below it.
- **Library lounge:** a looping LED ring on a dark timber ceiling, bronze fins
  with a cove light, a fluted-oak backlit bar niche, a bronze tube screen and
  curved bouclé seating. The Rainforest-stone fireplace plinth stays.
- **Kitchen:** now follows the travertine and onyx island reference, with the
  branching globe chandelier as its sculptural piece.
- **Dining:** a dark marble table and a **dark Emperador marble floor** (this
  zone only), a full-height bottle wall between labradorite slabs, and two
  textured-glass lantern pendants. A curved oak and green marble sideboard is
  added.
- **Entrance:** a dark textured stone wall with a gold ribbon light, a
  leaf-perforated metal screen, a curved dark stone inlay in the pale floor,
  and a bronze slatted display screen over a curved oak cabinet.
- **Palette:** green marble (Verde Guatemala or similar) is added as a second
  rare accent next to teal.
